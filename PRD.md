# HCM Lite Product Requirements

**Status:** Implemented product requirements
**Last aligned:** 30 October 2026

## 1. Product

HCM Lite is a multi-tenant human capital management system for Admin, HR and Employee workflows. It starts with empty workspaces, keeps business calculations on the server, and offers an optional AI assistant with the same role boundaries as the core API.

## 2. Roles

| Role | Scope | Main permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | One company workspace | Configure the company, manage access, view company data, finalize payroll and review audit logs |
| HR | One company workspace | Manage employee records, attendance, leave and payroll drafts |
| Employee | Own profile | View and update permitted profile details, attendance, leave, payslips and personal dashboard |

## 3. Functional scope

1. Authentication and access: company signup, cookie sessions, role guards, temporary-password onboarding, forced password changes and single-use reset links.
2. Organization: company settings, departments, designations, leave types and holidays.
3. Employees: directory, Employee 360, salary history and CSV import/export with validation.
4. Attendance: check-in/out, optional camera and geofence checks, correction requests and company board.
5. Leave: requests, cancellation, balances, working-day calculation, overlap checks and approvals.
6. Payroll: salary structures, monthly drafts, tax and attendance deductions, Admin finalization and PDF payslips.
7. Analytics: role-specific dashboards, trends, department metrics and payroll/leave summaries.
8. AI: role-scoped chat tools, dashboard summaries, smart leave parsing and policy answers.
9. Communication: role-targeted announcements, notifications and auditable actions.

## 4. Business rules

### Attendance

- Check-in and check-out times are generated by the server.
- Only one attendance record is allowed per employee per day.
- Late status uses the configured office start time and grace period (defaults: 09:00 and 15 minutes).
- Half-day status uses the configured threshold (default: 5 hours).
- Payroll treats missing working-day attendance as absent unless approved leave covers the date.

### Leave

- Working days exclude configured weekends and active company holidays.
- Signup creates Annual (20), Sick (10), Casual (10), and Unpaid leave types by default. Unpaid leave does not consume a quota.
- Requests are checked for overlap and available paid-leave balance. Balance changes follow approval/cancellation outcomes.

### Payroll

- Salary components derive from the employee's basic salary; calculations run on the backend.
- Payroll accounts for employment dates, working days, holidays, absences, unpaid leave, half days, late penalties and progressive annual tax slabs.
- Only Admin can finalize a draft. Finalized runs are locked.

## 5. Workspace onboarding and security

1. An organization creates its workspace at `/signup`; signup creates the company, first Admin and default leave types.
2. Admin configures organization settings and creates HR accounts.
3. HR registers employees; temporary passwords must be changed at first sign-in.
4. Tenant-owned records are scoped to the authenticated workspace. API authorization is enforced server-side.

Sessions use a signed JWT in an httpOnly cookie. Passwords are hashed. AI credentials remain on the backend. Audit snapshots redact credential fields. Production errors do not expose internal diagnostics. No global first-run takeover or seeded demo workspace is provided.

## 6. API areas

The Express API exposes `/api/auth`, `/api/users`, `/api/settings`, `/api/departments`, `/api/designations`, `/api/leave-types`, `/api/holidays`, `/api/employees`, `/api/attendance`, `/api/attendance-corrections`, `/api/leave`, `/api/payroll`, `/api/dashboard`, `/api/ai`, `/api/announcements`, `/api/notifications`, and `/api/audit-logs`.
